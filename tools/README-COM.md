## qcode

A QR code builder.

I was looking for a good QR code generator and noticed that all the decent
ones are paid. So I made my own.

## What's inside

Three tabs:

**qcode** — the gallery. All your saved codes live here: see a preview, open,
rename, delete or download any of them.

**ai** — a tab for working with an AI. The "Copy instructions" button puts a
description of every parameter, plus the current code, on your clipboard.

- Paste it into your AI (any one will do)
- Describe the QR design you want in plain words
- Get a JSON back
- Paste it in and hit "Apply"

**manual** — the same settings, by hand.


`ai` and `manual` are the same code, just two ways to build it.
Switch between them as you like — nothing gets lost.
